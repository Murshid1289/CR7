Historical Heritage

SUPER BALLON D'OR VS B.O.A.T.

Understanding the distinction between France Football's legendary 1989 jury accolade and the modern 2026 worldwide fan vote.

DECEMBER 24, 1989 HISTORICAL ONCE-EVER

The Super Ballon d'Or

Awarded only once in history by France Football to celebrate the 30th anniversary of the European Cup. Alfredo Di Stéfano was selected over Johan Cruyff and Michel Platini through a combination of jury journalists, former winners, and readers.

Winner: Alfredo Di Stéfano (Real Madrid)
Physical Trophy: Yes, special golden trophy with red rubies
Eligible: Only European/Naturalized European Players
OCTOBER 1, 2026 CURRENT FAN VOTE

The B.O.A.T. (Ballon d'Or of All Time)

A global public digital engagement vote organized by France Football and UEFA for the 70th Ballon d'Or anniversary. Rather than whole careers, fans vote for the single greatest Ballon d'Or-winning season from multi-time champions.

Inaugural Winner: Cristiano Ronaldo (2008 Season)
Decision Method: 100% Worldwide Public Fan Ballot
